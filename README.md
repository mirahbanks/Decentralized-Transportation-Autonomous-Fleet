# Decentralized Transportation Autonomous Fleet

A blockchain-based smart contract system for managing autonomous vehicle fleets in a decentralized transportation network. This system provides transparent, automated coordination of self-driving vehicles while ensuring safety, efficiency, and proper maintenance through distributed consensus.

## Overview

The Decentralized Transportation Autonomous Fleet consists of five interconnected smart contracts that work together to create a fully autonomous, self-managing transportation system. By leveraging blockchain technology, the system eliminates the need for centralized fleet operators while maintaining high standards of safety and efficiency.

## System Architecture

### Core Components

- **Vehicle Verification Contract**: Handles the registration and validation of autonomous vehicles
- **Fleet Coordination Contract**: Manages vehicle deployment and assignment across the network
- **Route Optimization Contract**: Calculates and assigns optimal routes for maximum efficiency
- **Safety Monitoring Contract**: Continuously tracks vehicle performance and safety metrics
- **Maintenance Scheduling Contract**: Automates preventive and corrective maintenance scheduling

## Smart Contracts

### 1. Vehicle Verification Contract

**Purpose**: Validates and registers autonomous vehicles joining the fleet network.

**Key Functions**:
- Vehicle registration and certification validation
- Hardware and software compatibility checks
- Insurance and compliance verification
- Periodic re-certification processes
- Vehicle status management (active, inactive, maintenance)

**Validation Criteria**:
- Autonomous driving system certification
- Safety sensor functionality
- Communication system compatibility
- Insurance coverage verification
- Regulatory compliance confirmation

### 2. Fleet Coordination Contract

**Purpose**: Orchestrates vehicle deployment and manages fleet-wide operations.

**Key Functions**:
- Real-time vehicle availability tracking
- Demand-based vehicle allocation
- Geographic zone management
- Load balancing across service areas
- Emergency vehicle redeployment

**Coordination Features**:
- Dynamic vehicle assignment based on demand patterns
- Multi-modal transportation integration
- Peak hour optimization
- Service area coverage monitoring
- Vehicle utilization analytics

### 3. Route Optimization Contract

**Purpose**: Calculates and assigns optimal routes for autonomous vehicles.

**Key Functions**:
- Real-time traffic analysis and route calculation
- Multi-vehicle route coordination
- Energy efficiency optimization
- Passenger pickup/dropoff optimization
- Dynamic rerouting based on conditions

**Optimization Algorithms**:
- Shortest path algorithms with traffic weighting
- Energy consumption minimization
- Passenger wait time reduction
- Vehicle clustering for efficiency
- Predictive routing based on historical data

### 4. Safety Monitoring Contract

**Purpose**: Continuously monitors vehicle performance and safety metrics.

**Key Functions**:
- Real-time safety metric collection
- Incident detection and response
- Performance threshold monitoring
- Safety score calculation and tracking
- Automated safety alerts and interventions

**Monitoring Parameters**:
- Vehicle sensor status and accuracy
- Driving behavior analysis
- System response times
- Environmental condition adaptation
- Passenger safety indicators

### 5. Maintenance Scheduling Contract

**Purpose**: Manages preventive and corrective maintenance for the fleet.

**Key Functions**:
- Predictive maintenance scheduling
- Service appointment coordination
- Parts inventory management
- Maintenance history tracking
- Cost optimization for repairs

**Maintenance Features**:
- Mileage-based maintenance triggers
- Condition-based maintenance alerts
- Seasonal maintenance scheduling
- Emergency repair coordination
- Maintenance provider network management
